Owner: Juan
Traceability: US-004

## Spatial rule

HomeLab is one physically coherent, continuous environment. Visitors normally walk between areas. The only primary energy portal is the entrance from the forest. Normal sections do not use teleportation unless a later accepted spec changes the model.

## Canonical sequence

`Forest approach → Main energy portal → Central Atrium → Bridges over water → Glass pavilions`

The atrium is the orientation hub, not a menu disguised as a room. Sightlines, paths, landmarks, signage, and Zavit support navigation.

## Coherence constraints

- Pavilion connections must read as plausible walking routes.
- Water and bridges shape circulation; they must not become decorative blockers.
- Major destinations need distinct silhouettes and visible orientation cues.
- Indoor/outdoor transitions preserve awareness of nature.
- Scale must support both cinematic composition and readable interaction.
- Loading boundaries may be technical, but must not appear as unexplained teleportation.
- Mobile/adapted modes may simplify navigation while preserving the same information architecture.

## Validation for future slices

Before a world slice is accepted, evidence must show:

- the visitor can identify the next destination without trial-and-error;
- paths contain no unintended dead ends or collision traps;
- architecture, water, vegetation, and interaction targets read at intended camera distances;
- keyboard, reduced-motion, mobile, and semantic alternatives preserve access to content;
- continuity survives lazy loading and degraded rendering.

Exact layout, dimensions, collision model, traversal method, and loading transitions are UNKNOWN pending US-010 refinement and prototypes.
