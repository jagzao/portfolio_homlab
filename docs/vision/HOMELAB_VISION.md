# HomeLab Vision

Status: ACCEPTED product direction
Owner: Juan (Product Owner)
Traceability: US-004

## Product promise

HomeLab is a living personal research laboratory that also functions as Juan's professional portfolio. It combines engineering, technology, nature, experimentation, curiosity, science, and creative exploration.

It is not a CV rendered in Three.js. The world must demonstrate credible engineering while letting a recruiter identify Juan's strengths quickly.

## Audiences and value

- Recruiters: understand professional identity, core technologies, and evidence without learning the interface.
- Senior engineers: inspect decisions, systems thinking, trade-offs, and verified project evidence.
- Curious visitors: explore a coherent place with useful guided and free paths.

Success requires both reactions: “Juan builds impressive things across many technologies” and “there is real engineering behind this.”

## Product principles

1. Demonstrate capability; do not create logo soup.
2. Preserve one continuous, walkable world with one entrance portal.
3. Make nature constantly present beside futuristic technology.
4. Keep professional claims verified, publishable, and data-driven.
5. Keep semantic professional content available outside WebGL.
6. Build small, inspectable vertical slices; Software Engineering is first.
7. Add infrastructure only for a real capability, with cost and security justified.
8. Treat visual quality, accessibility, and performance as acceptance criteria.

## Product boundaries

- Zavi is Juan's private AI/Second Brain ecosystem. Zavit is the HomeLab robot host.
- Visitors and Zavit never receive unrestricted private Second Brain access.
- No company, project, metric, technology, responsibility, architecture, SLA, user count, or outcome may be inferred as fact.
- Simulated operational values must be labeled as simulations.

## Success signals

Initial signals are PROVISIONAL until tested with visitors:

- A first-time recruiter can identify Juan's primary engineering identity and core stack within 60 seconds.
- A visitor can choose guided or free exploration and reach professional evidence without a forced tutorial.
- Every public professional claim maps to a verified, publishable source record.
- The same essential professional content is usable through semantic HTML without WebGL.
- Each shipped vertical slice meets its visual, accessibility, security, and performance budgets.

## Current foundation scope

This document establishes direction only. No 3D experience, backend, cloud infrastructure, or professional claim is implemented by US-004.
