# User Journey

Status: ACCEPTED journey intent; timings and detailed interactions PROVISIONAL
Traceability: US-004

## Journey goals

The experience must satisfy visitors who want a concise professional answer and visitors who want to explore. It cannot require 3D fluency to access Juan's work.

## Primary journey

1. **Load and orient.** A semantic shell identifies Juan and HomeLab while the experience initializes. No blank loading screen.
2. **Approach.** The visitor encounters the forest, HomeLab exterior, and the one main energy portal.
3. **Enter.** The portal transitions into the Central Atrium without implying portals between later areas.
4. **Meet an active world.** Zavit is performing an activity. The visitor can choose Guided Mode or Free Exploration.
5. **Understand options.** Atrium landmarks and semantic navigation expose major areas and an accessible portfolio mode.
6. **Walk to Software Engineering Lab.** The route crosses a bridge over water and preserves spatial continuity.
7. **Inspect evidence.** Architecture Table, Engineering Decisions Wall, Technology Wall, Current Workbench, and Flagship Projects communicate verified capability.
8. **Explore or conclude.** The visitor may continue through available areas or use direct semantic project/contact paths.

## Guided Mode

Guided Mode recommends a short route, explains why each stop matters, and permits skipping or exiting at any time. It must not seize navigation indefinitely or hide content behind dialogue.

## Free Exploration

Free Exploration keeps landmarks, labels, orientation, and accessible navigation available. Discovery is encouraged; essential professional information is not concealed as an easter egg.

## Recovery and alternate paths

- Unsupported WebGL, low-power hardware, reduced motion, mobile constraints, or user preference lead to an adapted or semantic experience.
- Loading failure exposes retry and semantic navigation.
- A visitor can return to orientation without restarting.
- Keyboard users can reach equivalent content and interactions.
- Direct links can open meaningful professional content without replaying arrival.

## Provisional journey acceptance targets

- Professional identity and primary stack are discoverable within 60 seconds in moderated testing.
- Mode choice is understandable without prior instruction.
- The visitor can reach Software Engineering evidence through 3D and semantic paths.
- No essential claim requires hover, sound, precise pointer control, or WebGL.
- Browser back/forward and deep links do not strand the visitor.

These targets require empirical validation after an executable vertical slice exists. No journey runtime is implemented in this foundation.
